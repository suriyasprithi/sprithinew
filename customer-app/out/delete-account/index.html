Delete your account

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SPRITHI account and the personal data associated with it. SPRITHI is a wallet-powered, multi-vendor marketplace for ordering products from local shops.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sprithi@gmail.com from the email address or mobile number registered on your SPRITHI account, with the subject “Account and data deletion request”.
  2. Include your registered name and mobile number so we can locate your account.
  3. You can also call us at +91 94420 25515 to make the request.
  4. We verify your identity and then delete your account. This is completed within 30 days of a verified request.

Email a deletion request

What data is deleted

Once your request is verified, we delete the personal data linked to your account, including:

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and date of birth.
  • Your saved delivery addresses and region (state/district/taluk/pincode).
  • Any KYC and bank details you added (PAN, PAN image, bank account, IFSC, UPI ID).
  • Your push-notification token and in-app activity profile.

What we keep, and for how long

Some records are retained only where the law requires it — for example completed order, payment, wallet and tax/accounting records, and information needed for fraud prevention. These are kept only for the period required by law and are then deleted. We do not use retained records for marketing.

Note: any remaining in-app wallet balance is forfeited on account deletion unless a withdrawal is completed beforehand. Please withdraw your balance before requesting deletion.
