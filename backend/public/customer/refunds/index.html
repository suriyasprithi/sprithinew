Returns & refunds

If an item you received is damaged, incorrect or missing, please raise a return request from your Orders page within the return window shown for that order, or contact us.

How returns work

  • Open the order and choose Request return on an eligible order.
  • The seller reviews your request and, once approved, the applicable amount is credited back to your SPRITHI wallet.
  • Perishable, used or final-sale items may not be eligible for return.

For any help with a return, reach Customer support.
